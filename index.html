Why now · November 2026

The world is running out of power faster than it can build it.

AI and cloud computing have turned electricity into the bottleneck of the digital economy. The grid can't connect new load fast enough, and households are paying the price. The next decade belongs to power that can be deployed locally, quickly and cleanly.

What this means for investors

Operators are already turning to on-site generation — gas turbines and fuel cells — because interconnection queues stretch for years. Each of those has trade-offs: fuel supply, emissions, noise, water. A compact, clean generator that can be placed beside the load addresses the single biggest constraint in the fastest-growing market in energy. That is the opportunity Quasar is built for.

The technology

Quasar | Power Source

Quasar is an engineered fluid-dynamics generator. It converts controlled flow into electricity through electromagnetic induction, drawing on principles every engineer knows: conservation of energy and mass, continuity of motion, Bernoulli's and Pascal's fluid principles.

No exotic claims. Quasar obeys the laws of physics — it is not perpetual motion. Its advantage comes from engineering: capturing and converting energy that conventional designs waste.

  • Weatherproof — designed to operate regardless of sun or wind.
  • Decentralized — energy made when and where it's needed, reducing dependence on long-distance transmission.
  • Clean — designed to substantially reduce carbon emissions versus fossil generation.
  • Serviceable — supported by a network of Certified Quasar Technicians.
